<h1>Mission</h1>

<p class="lede">At SUNDROP, we acknowledge that music is for everyone, and we strive to reduce barriers between artists and the stage. We foster a welcoming space for musicians to find their voices, experiment with new material, try out new venues, and rehearse for upcoming gigs in an inclusive environment.</p>
